Frequently Asked Questions

Questions Clients Often Ask

Who pays the Google or Meta ad budget?

The client generally pays the advertising spend separately. ScaleUp Media's management/service fee is separate from the actual advertising budget unless otherwise agreed in writing.

Do you guarantee leads, sales or rankings?

No. Marketing outcomes depend on multiple factors. We focus on strategy, implementation, testing and optimization, but specific results cannot be guaranteed.

Do you need our social media password?

Where possible, secure role-based account access is preferred over sharing passwords. Access requirements depend on the platform and agreed services.

How long should we work together?

Digital marketing often requires consistent testing and optimization. A minimum initial engagement period may be recommended depending on the scope and business objectives.

Do you create custom proposals?

Yes. After a discovery discussion and review, the recommended scope can be customized for the client's goals and requirements.
